WHATSAPP BUSINESS SENDER — WHAT DEFENDER OCTA ACTUALLY NEEDS
============================================================
Decided 23 Sep 2026. Residents are app-only: they log in with a flat
number and the PIN on their printed slip, no phone number is stored, and
everything reaches them as an app notification. WhatsApp is now only for
the people who ANSWER an emergency — the security head, the committee
and you — because they are not residents and have no resident app.

That cuts the templates to submit from seven to three, and removes the
authentication (login code) template, which is the slowest kind to get
approved.

Switches, per site, in site_config.json:
    "resident_whatsapp":  false     (default) nothing to residents
    "resident_otp_login": false     (default) no WhatsApp login codes
Set either to true for a society that specifically wants it — the two
optional templates at the end of this file cover that case.
